Archive backfill

Repairs archived documents imported without their text (Pocket migration, bare RSS items, Android shares). Each one is deleted and re-created with the page content, fetched with your browser session. Runs once, at your request: expect 20 to 45 minutes, a few tabs opening, and some failures on dead links or paywalls you do not subscribe to.
